The McDowell Sonoran Conservancy

Explore the beauty of the desert at the McDowell Sonoran Preserve, where scenic hiking trails, diverse wildlife, native plants, and stunning vistas await.

  • Location: 23015 N 128th Street, Scottsdale, AZ 85255
  • Hours: Daily, from sunrise to sunset.
  • Cost: Trails are free.
  • Pet Friendly: Yes, dogs allowed on trailheads.

Desert Botanical Garden

This curated outdoor space is home to thousands of desert plants, colorful seasonal blooms, and walking trails that showcase the region's unique natural landscape.

  • Location: 1201 N Galvin Parkway, Phoenix, AZ 85008
  • Hours: Daily, from 7am - 10pm.
  • Cost: $32.95 for adults, $16.95 for kids.
  • Pet Friendly: Dogs allowed for special events.

Tacos Chiwas

Savor delicious Chihuahua-style Mexican street food at this Phoenix favorite known for its handmade tortillas, flavorful tacos, and delicious regional specialties.

  • Location: 1028 E Indian School Rd, Phoenix, AZ 85014
  • Hours: Mon-Sat, 10am - 9pm. Sunday, 10am-4pm.
  • Cost: $3.49 per taco, $10 per burrito, $6 per quesadilla.
  • Pet Friendly: Service dogs allowed inside.
