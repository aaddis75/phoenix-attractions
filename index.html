Phoenix Attractions

The McDowell Sonoran Conservancy

Explore the Sonoran Desert at the McDowell Sonoran Preserve, where scenic hiking trails, diverse wildlife, native plants, and stunning desert landscapes await visitors daily from sunrise to sunset, free of charge.

  • Location: 23015 N 128th Street, Scottsdale, AZ 85255
  • Hours: Daily, from sunrise to sunset.
  • Cost: Trails are free.
  • Pet Friendly: Yes, dogs allowed on trailheads.
Visit the McDowell Sonoran Conservancy website

Desert Botanical Garden

Discover the beauty of the Sonoran Desert at the Desert Botanical Garden, home to thousands of fascinating desert plants, colorful seasonal blooms, and inviting trails that celebrate the region's unique natural landscape.

  • Location: 1201 N. Galvin Parkway, Phoenix, AZ 85008
  • Hours: Daily, from 7am - 10pm.
  • Cost: $32.95 for adults, $16.95 for kids.
  • Pet Friendly: Dogs allowed for special events.
Visit the Desert Botanical Garden website

Tacos Chiwas

Savor authentic Chihuahua-style Mexican street food at this Phoenix favorite known for its handmade tortillas, flavorful tacos, and delicious regional specialties.

  • Location: 1028 East Indian School Road, Phoenix, AZ 85014
  • Hours: Monday-Saturday, from 10am - 9pm. Sunday 10am-4pm.
  • Cost: $3.49 per taco, $10 per burrito, $6 per quesadilla.
  • Pet Friendly: Dogs not allowed inside.
Visit the Tacos Chiwas website
